About Program Teenspace

A catalyst for the next generation of creators.

We believe that youth possess untamed creativity and potential. Program Teenspace provides the environment, tools, and mentorship needed to turn raw ideas into real-world impact.

Whether you're interested in technology, arts, leadership, or entrepreneurship, our spaces are designed to support your unique journey in a safe, dynamic, and inclusive community.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can attend Program Teenspace?

Program Teenspace is specially curated for Higher Secondary (Plus One & Plus Two) students looking to explore creativity, tech, leadership, and personal development.

How can I register for the conference?

Click on any "Register Now" button on this website, fill out the simple registration form, and submit your details.

Where is the event venue located?

The event takes place at Mujahid Centre, Wisdom Office, Kozhikode North. You can click the location card above to navigate via Google Maps.
